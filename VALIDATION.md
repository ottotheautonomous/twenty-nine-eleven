# Verification — 10 October 2026

The Sentinel revision replaces the Threshold doorway and grid floor with a geodesic perimeter of light around an ember, an interaction model built on igloo.inc's documented pointer behaviour, and non-interactive capability rows. Direction: a Fable-overseen research team, four competing Fable concepts, two judges and a Fable spec; implementation by Claude Opus. Prior versions remain in Git history.

- Production build: passed. One JS bundle, about 64 kB gzip (spec budget 75 kB). No new npm dependencies; the scene code is raw WebGL2 lifted from a reviewed kit with its licences in `public/third-party-notices.txt`.
- Backend tests: 18 passed, with stubbed email delivery. No test email sent.
- Form validation, payload, submission, success/error copy and "Send another" are byte-identical to the previous revision. Dialog markup is unchanged.
- Every scene input is a uniform written by Motion (`threeEffect`) or by the CPU model; the loop runs on Motion's `frame.update`/`frame.render`. No `requestAnimationFrame` in the source.
- WebGL2 (SwiftShader, `?gl=force`) and Canvas2D renders were inspected at 1440×900, 390×844 and 320×568: lattice centred on the DOM anchor, ember at the centre, no floor or grid, type on ink, no horizontal overflow, both rows on one line.
- Pointer: hairlines lock onto at most four nodes; at most three readouts decode in, unique, from the documentation-range pool only; the corner line switches to `TRACKING` and integrity dips and recovers. Integrity is a ledger (pure-model simulation at 30/60/120/144 Hz, within 0.013 of target): a slow 180 px/s pass stays 0.998 with no event; a press 0.915 and heals silently; one fast pass 0.886, a brisk one 0.860, two fast passes 0.737, a 3 s scrub 0.392, an 8 s scrub 0.142. One second after the last paint the reset fires, the deficit holds until 0.567 s later, releases 35 % in one step and heals with a 0.85 s half-life. The trail grid ticks at a fixed 60 Hz, so a gesture costs the same on 120/144 Hz screens. Row hover keeps its letter wave, underline and separator response, sends a ripple through the lattice, lights the asset class (row 1) or tightens the lattice (row 2).
- Rows: plain `span`s, `tabIndex -1`, no click handler; a click opens nothing; tab order is contact → verse → pause. Hover text never moves (0.000 px letter movement measured on every row; v1 lifted letters 3.18 px).
- Verse panel: the colophon is a button that opens Jeremiah 29:11 (Berean Standard Bible, verified against the BSB's official text files) in the contact panel's glass and spring. The ember warms and the lattice holds still; words take the light in reading order after the panel lands and move 0 px within it. Focus goes to the verse, Tab stays on the close button, Escape and backdrop close and return focus to the colophon. The colophon's glyphs sit exactly where they did (0.0 px at four viewports). The contact form code is byte-identical.
- Modal: the panel springs from the ember; the lattice breathes, dims and draws back; no slot or gate opens.
- Fallbacks forced in a test browser: without WebGL the Canvas2D tier draws the same lattice, ember, links and response; without any canvas the WebP poster of the real settled frame is shown and the pause control is hidden.
- Reduced motion: no entrance, idle, camera motion or pulses; one composed still; pause control hidden. Pause stops everything and resumes cleanly.
- Review round (Fable-overseen; 21 findings, each confirmed by an adversarial verifier, plus a Fable creative review) applied in 6837707: presence-gated proximity (the idle frame is at rest), tear = one fast pass (pure-model simulation: two fast swipes earn the finding at 60 and 30 Hz; slow 1.5 s sweeps never tear and integrity stays 0.998), unique readouts capped at three with AA plates, gesture uniforms that restart from keyframes, a governor that starts at the DPR cap and reads the real refresh, still frames that are seeded and clean, pinch-zoom kept, cheaper resize and style work.

- v1.2 (Fable-overseen acceptance; deterministic 60 fps captures on SwiftShader, pure-model simulations at 30-144 Hz):

  - The hand: measured across the view ray, so the face under the pointer feels it (v1.1 moved it 0.0 px). A 1000 px/s pass changes 958-4,745 px of the cage vs hand off (v1.1: 229-700); a 180 px/s reading pass 300-670 px.
  - Integrity ledger: slow pass .998 (no event), click .915 (silent at all 97 tested positions), fast pass .886, brisk .860, two passes .737, 3 s scrub .392, 8 s scrub .142, within .013 at 30/60/72/75/120/144 Hz. One second after the last stroke the reset fires: the ember flares, RESTORING decodes, a ring leaves the ember across the disc and seals the breach, the reading counts up once (35 % of the deficit) as it clears the limb, then heals with a 0.85 s half-life. An interrupted reset keeps what it healed. Tears and the finding are unchanged.
  - Bloom budget (HDR read-back, 4x4 blocks in the 0.5-1.2 R annulus): idle max 0.544 (ember only); brisk passes <= 2 blocks over 0.8; press <= 1; the reset ring <= 3; a governor row-shed changes light by <= 1.9 % per frame.
